# Egg 0.1.5

The glass release.

- **The shelf moves again.** Dragging the header, the empty background, or
  any edge works everywhere — clicks no longer fall through the glass to
  the window behind it.
- **Purely visual cards.** The pile gives its whole surface to your images;
  names and details live in the rows and their hover preview.
- **The Active band.** A pill on the shelf opens your running world: live
  sandboxes with uptime, the dev servers on this Mac (open, quit, or take
  one into a sandbox), and the prototypes a click from booting.
- **The library window wears the glass.** Transparent window, floating
  native toolbar, content scrolling beneath — and menus across the app
  picked up icons.
- Empty categories are drop targets again, the selection bar speaks
  Music-style icons, and a stack of small paddings, truncations, and hover
  states got their pass.
