# Egg 0.1.6

The tester release: Egg now assumes nothing about your setup.

- **A new app icon.** The egg, properly drawn, in every size macOS asks for.
- **Sandboxes are opt-in.** A fresh Egg keeps everything on your Mac —
  capture, shelf, library, feed — and sends are a local echo. Turn
  sandboxes on in Settings when you have a heyvm daemon or a heyo key of
  your own. **Existing setups: flip the new "Enable sandboxes" toggle once
  to get your sandboxes back.**
- **The menu-bar egg always comes back.** If it was ever dragged off the
  menu bar, macOS remembered forever; now every launch puts it back.
- **The shelf reads on any desktop.** Dark mode gets a dark wash behind
  the glass so white text survives a bright background; light mode's
  buttons sit in grey containers with quieter icons.
- **The shelf hugs a small pile.** One or two cards no longer float in a
  fixed-height area — the panel shrinks to fit and grows back as cards
  arrive.
- The item-count pill ("1 Image") lives on the left beside the plus,
  with the other bands on the right.
